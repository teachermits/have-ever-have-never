Phase 1: Concept & Formula Blueprint

Present Perfect for Life Experiences

Use the Present Perfect tense to talk about experiences in your life up to right now. We do not specify the exact time when something happened.

1

Asking About Experiences

Question
Have + you + EVER + V3 (Past Participle) + ... ?

Example:

"Have you ever flown on a helicopter?"

2

Zero Experience ("Never")

Negative
I + HAVE + NEVER + V3 (Past Participle) + ...

Example:

"I have never eaten raw fish."

3

Affirmative Short Answer

Yes Response
Yes, I have. (Optional details in Past Simple: "I visited Paris in 2022.")

💡 Key Rule: Do not use contractions in short positive answers (Say "Yes, I have", NOT "Yes, I've").

4

Negative Short Answer

No Response
No, I haven't. (Or full negative: "No, I have never tried it.")

💡 Key Rule: "Haven't" is the contraction of "have not". "Never" makes the sentence negative automatically.

Regular & Irregular Quick Reference
